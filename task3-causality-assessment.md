Task 3: Causality Assessment

## Objective
To evaluate the relationship between a suspected drug and an adverse drug reaction (ADR) using standard causality assessment criteria.

## Case Study

- Patient: 45-year-old Female
- Suspected Drug: Amoxicillin
- Adverse Reaction: Skin Rash
- Onset: 3 days after starting Amoxicillin
- Outcome: Symptoms improved after stopping the drug

## Causality Assessment

### 1. Temporal Relationship
The skin rash appeared 3 days after starting Amoxicillin. This supports a possible relationship between the drug and the reaction.

### 2. Dechallenge
The symptoms improved after Amoxicillin was stopped. This supports a drug-related reaction.

### 3. Alternative Causes
No other clear cause for the skin rash was identified from the given case information.

## Assessment

Based on the temporal relationship, positive dechallenge, and absence of a clear alternative cause, the reaction is classified as:

**PROBABLE**

## Conclusion
Amoxicillin is the suspected drug responsible for the skin rash. The causality assessment indicates a probable relationship between Amoxicillin and the adverse reaction.
